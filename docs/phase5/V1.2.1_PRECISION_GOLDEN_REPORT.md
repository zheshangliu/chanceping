## Precision controls verified

- Trigger classifier rejects evergreen careers pages, corporate history,
  treasury/service pages, stale news, and mismatched entities before signal
  persistence.
- Entity post-filter rejects HKEX vs HK Express, Bankcomm vs BOCHK,
  HSBC China vs HSBC India, and Protiviti HK vs Protiviti India; jobs require
  an employer/domain binding and compatible geography.
- Official contact extraction recovers `recruit@hkib.org` and
  `group@gba-pharma.com` from first-party content while rejecting
  `privacy@`, `webmaster@`, `support@`, and unrelated media mailboxes.
- Contact search budgets remain bounded (Serper 3, Exa 2, official site 3
  per company) and unknown costs are never converted to zero.
