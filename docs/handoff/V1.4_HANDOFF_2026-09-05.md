## Architecture Impact

- **API:** Added `/opportunities` and `/watchlist` Finance routes, including list/detail and watchlist operations, and mounted them through the HeadHunter API.
- **Data model:** Added persisted `OpportunityRecord` and `WatchlistEntry` JSON stores. Opportunity lifecycle is explicit (`discovered` → `verified` → `ready_to_contact` → `contacted` → `in_follow_up` → `completed`/`archived`) and human review is represented separately.
- **Pipeline:** Weekly execution now materializes only valid, gated signals into opportunities and updates watchlist snapshot metadata. No fake seed records are used.
- **UI:** `/weekly` is the Pacific Executive Brief view, with dark hero, gold accents, signal ribbon, opportunity feed, and navigation to Opportunities and Watchlist.
- **Provider/routing:** TikHub is not called by the V1.4 weekly route; existing production provider routing is unchanged.

## Business Impact

Wayne can open the public Finance weekly brief, see the current week's machine-generated opportunity feed, and follow the path into opportunities and watchlist records. The full live run produced 21 gated leads (1 A / 20 B) from 18 themes, with 4 valid signals; the current data is evidence for evaluation, not a claim that every lead is contact-ready. A-level records require the existing contact gate before `ready_to_contact`.
