## Architecture Impact

- Data structure: added the persisted `business_driver_zh` read-model field; existing lead, evidence, contact, run and snapshot stores remain backward compatible.
- API: no provider or routing change; Finance GET APIs stay public, while Finance mutations and global legacy high-cost mutations return `401 FINANCE_PUBLIC_READ_ONLY` without a session.
- Pipeline: weekly snapshots continue to flow through eligibility, evidence/contact gates, lead ranking and publication; failed snapshots cannot replace the current published snapshot.
- UI: Finance cards now expose Business Driver and explicit Fact/Inference/BD Hypothesis layers, with responsive four-to-one column behavior.

## Business Impact

Compared with V1.4, Wayne can now review a ranked weekly opportunity queue, inspect why a company is actionable, distinguish evidence from inference and BD hypothesis, open public contact paths, copy a first-touch script, and use the existing authenticated workflow to record follow-up. The public page remains safe to browse without spending provider budget or mutating shared state.
