## Implemented

- Executive navigation is localized to 周报 / 机会池 / 关注公司 / 公司库 / 管理.
- Weekly Brief now leads with “今天联系谁？” and a Top 3 “今日行动” panel before the opportunity list.
- Homepage cards show company, level, Why Now, contact and next action first; evidence and judgment are behind “展开证据与判断”.
- Fact, inference and BD hypothesis labels are localized to 事实依据 / 系统判断 / 业务建议; Business Driver is presented as 业务动因.
- Operator funnel/provider-style information is removed from the homepage and retained under the 管理 / 雷达运行 view.
- Added `/opportunities/:opportunityId` detail route with company profile, Why Now, evidence chain, talent need, recommended contacts, first-touch copy, status update and follow-up note form.
- Added a published-Lead fallback so weekly opportunities remain actionable even when the separate opportunity index has no materialized rows; fallback details use stable `lead-*` IDs.
- Added persisted optional `follow_up_notes` to OpportunityRecord; existing mutation authentication remains unchanged.
